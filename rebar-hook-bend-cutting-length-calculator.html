How to use: Rebar Hook, Bend & Cutting Length Calculator

  1. Enter the straight (out-to-out) length.
  2. Enter the number of hooks and each type of bend.
  3. Read the cutting length in mm and m.

Formula

Cutting length = straight length + (hooks × 9d) − (45°×1d + 90°×2d + 135°×3d).

Rebar Hook, Bend & Cutting Length Calculator — FAQ

What is the hook length of rebar?

A standard 180° hook adds about 9 × bar diameter to the cutting length.

Why do we deduct for bends?

Bending a bar shortens its outside length, so a deduction (1d–3d) is applied per bend.
