Rebar Tonnage Calculator

Convert quantities of bars into tonnage for ordering and pricing. Enter the bar size, length and quantity to get metric tons, US tons and total kilograms.

How to use it

  1. Pick the bar size.
  2. Enter length per bar and quantity.
  3. Read the tonnage.

Formula

Weight (kg) = kg/m × length × quantity. Metric ton = kg ÷ 1000.

Questions

How many bars in a ton of rebar?

Divide 1000 by (kg/m × bar length). For 12 mm × 12 m bars, about 94 bars per ton.

What is the difference between metric and US tons?

A metric ton is 1000 kg; a US (short) ton is 907.2 kg.
