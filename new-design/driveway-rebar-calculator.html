Driveway Rebar Calculator

Size the rebar mat for a driveway. Enter the driveway length and width, the bar spacing and cover, and get the bar count both ways plus the total steel weight.

How to use it

  1. Enter driveway length and width.
  2. Set the bar spacing and edge cover.
  3. Pick the bar size for the total.

Formula

Bars each way = floor((dimension − 2 × cover) ÷ spacing) + 1, × kg/m for the weight.

Questions

What size rebar for a driveway?

#3–#4 (10–13 mm) at 300–450 mm centres is common for residential driveways.

Do driveways need rebar?

Reinforcement helps control cracking; thickness and bar size depend on loading.
