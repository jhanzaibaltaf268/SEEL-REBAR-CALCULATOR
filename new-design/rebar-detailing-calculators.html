Rebar Detailing & Lap Calculators

Engineering detailing tools for rebar development length (Ld), lap splices, hooks, bends, stirrup cutting lengths, chairs, and tie wire.

  • Rebar Spacing CalculatorFind the number of bars and the actual centre-to-centre spacing across any width, plus the clear gap between bars.
  • Rebar Development Length CalculatorCalculate the development (anchorage) length Ld for deformed bars using the IS 456 formula, with selectable concrete and steel grade.
  • Rebar Lap / Splice Length CalculatorCalculate lap (splice) length for reinforcement: tension lap = max(Ld, 30d), compression lap = max(Ld, 24d).
  • Rebar Hook, Bend & Cutting Length CalculatorCalculate the cutting length of a bar from its straight length, hooks and bends, with standard 9d hooks and bend deductions.
  • Stirrup & Tie Cutting Length CalculatorCalculate the cutting length of a rectangular stirrup or column tie including hooks and bend deductions.
  • Rebar Chair / Bar Support CalculatorCalculate how many bar chairs (supports) are needed to hold a top reinforcement mat on a square grid.
  • Rebar Tie Wire CalculatorEstimate binding (tie) wire for a reinforcement grid from slab size, spacing and wire used per tie.
