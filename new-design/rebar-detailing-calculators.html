Rebar Detailing & Lap Calculators

Engineering detailing tools for rebar development length (Ld), lap splices, hooks, bends, stirrup cutting lengths, chairs, and tie wire.

  • Rebar Spacing CalculatorBars across any width with the true centre-to-centre spacing and the clear gap between bars. No rounding surprises — see the exact bar count.
  • Rebar Development Length CalculatorAnchorage length Ld for deformed bars by the IS 456 formula, with concrete and steel grade options and bar-size tables. Free, with worked examples.
  • Rebar Lap / Splice Length CalculatorCorrect lap (splice) length every time: tension lap = max(Ld, 30d), compression lap = max(Ld, 24d). Free calculator with bar schedules. Try it free.
  • Rebar Hook, Bend & Cutting Length CalculatorCutting length from straight length, standard 9d hooks and bend deductions. Get the exact length to cut in seconds, free, with worked examples.
  • Stirrup & Tie Cutting Length CalculatorCutting length for rectangular stirrups and column ties, hooks and bend deductions included. Defaults: a 300 × 450 mm tie = 1,364 mm. Free, no signup.
  • Rebar Chair / Bar Support CalculatorHow many bar chairs hold your top mat? Count and spacing on a square grid in seconds — defaults 5 × 4 m at 1.0 m spacing = 30 chairs. Free calculator.
  • Rebar Tie Wire CalculatorBinding wire for any grid: tie count, total metres and kg from slab size and spacing. Defaults: 918 ties, 275.40 m of wire, 4.34 kg. Free, no signup.
