Concrete Slab Rebar Calculator

Work out exactly how much reinforcement your slab needs. Enter the slab dimensions, bar spacing and edge cover, and the calculator returns the bar count in each direction, total bar length, tie-wire intersections and the total steel weight.

How to use it

  1. Enter slab length and width.
  2. Set the bar spacing (centre-to-centre) and edge cover.
  3. Pick the bar size to get the bar count and total weight.
  4. Read the results: bar count in each direction, total bar length, tie intersections and total weight.
  5. Order extra for laps and cutting waste — laps are not included in this calculator (see the lap length calculator).

Formula

Bars one way = floor((dimension − 2 × cover) ÷ spacing) + 1, repeated for both directions. Total weight = total bar length × kg/m.

Questions

How much rebar do I need for a concrete slab?

Divide each slab dimension (less cover) by the spacing, add one, and do this both ways. Multiply each row by the bar length and the bar's kg/m.

What spacing should slab rebar be?

Residential slabs commonly use 150–300 mm centres; always follow your structural design.

How do I calculate slab rebar weight by hand?

Count the bars each way with floor((dimension − 2 × cover) ÷ spacing) + 1, multiply each count by its bar length, add both directions, then multiply the total length by the bar's kg/m.

What bar size is used for a house slab?

10 mm and 12 mm bars are the most common for residential slabs, but the size and spacing must come from your structural design.

Do I need to add extra for laps and wastage?

Yes. This calculator gives the neat grid weight. Bars longer than the available stock length need laps, and cutting always wastes some steel — add both on top of the calculated weight before ordering.

Can I use this calculator for a driveway slab?

Yes, the grid maths is the same. For a driveway, the dedicated driveway rebar calculator starts from typical driveway sizes.

How much rebar is in a 10 m × 10 m slab?

At 200 mm spacing, 50 mm cover and 12 mm bars: 50 bars × 9.9 m each way, 990 m total, 879.1 kg, and 2,500 tie intersections.

How much does the rebar for a slab cost?

Multiply the total weight by your local price per kg. Example: 209.6 kg × €1.20/kg = €251.52. Prices vary by region, supplier and bar size, so get a current quote.

Does the calculator include laps and hooks?

No. It gives the neat grid weight only. Laps, hooks, bends and cutting waste must be added separately before ordering.

What is edge cover and why does it matter?

Edge cover is the concrete between the bar ends and the slab edge. It protects the steel from corrosion and fire and holds the bars in position. The calculator subtracts twice the cover from each slab dimension before counting bars.
