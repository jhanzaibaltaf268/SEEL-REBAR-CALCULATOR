How to use: Rebar Chair / Bar Support Calculator

  1. Enter slab length and width.
  2. Set the chair grid spacing.
  3. Read the total chairs required.

Formula

Chairs = (floor(L ÷ spacing) + 1) × (floor(W ÷ spacing) + 1).

Rebar Chair / Bar Support Calculator — FAQ

How many rebar chairs do I need?

Roughly one per square metre at 1 m spacing — the tool gives the exact grid count.

What spacing for bar chairs?

Commonly 1.0–1.2 m each way for slab top mats.
