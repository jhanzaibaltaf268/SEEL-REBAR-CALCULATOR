How to use: Swimming Pool Rebar Calculator

  1. Enter the pool wall length and height.
  2. Set vertical and horizontal spacing.
  3. Pick bar sizes for the total per wall.

Formula

Same grid method as a wall: bars each way from spacing, summed × kg/m. Repeat per wall and add the floor.

Swimming Pool Rebar Calculator — FAQ

What size rebar for a pool?

#3–#4 (10–13 mm) at 250–300 mm centres each way is typical; follow your engineer.

How do I calculate pool rebar?

Calculate each wall panel and the floor separately, then add them together.
