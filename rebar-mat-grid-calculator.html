How to use: Rebar Mat & Grid Calculator

  1. Enter mat length and width.
  2. Set the grid spacing and edge offset.
  3. Pick a bar size for the totals.

Formula

Bars each way = floor((dimension − 2 × offset) ÷ spacing) + 1. Intersections = nx × ny.

Rebar Mat & Grid Calculator — FAQ

What is a rebar mat?

A grid of bars tied together in two directions, used in slabs, rafts and footings.

How many tie points are in a mat?

One at each bar intersection — the calculator reports this as the intersection count.
