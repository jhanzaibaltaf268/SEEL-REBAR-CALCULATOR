How to use: Footing Rebar Calculator

  1. Enter footing length and width.
  2. Set spacing and clear cover.
  3. Choose the bar size for counts and weight.

Formula

Same grid method as a slab: bars one way = floor((dimension − 2 × cover) ÷ spacing) + 1, both directions, × kg/m.

Footing Rebar Calculator — FAQ

What size rebar is used in footings?

10–16 mm (#4–#5) bars are typical for residential footings; design governs.

How do I calculate footing reinforcement?

Treat the footing as a small slab — bar count each way times bar length times the unit weight.
