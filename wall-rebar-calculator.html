How to use: Wall Rebar Calculator

  1. Enter wall length and height.
  2. Set vertical and horizontal spacing and cover.
  3. Pick bar sizes for each direction.

Formula

Vertical bars = floor((L − 2cover) ÷ spV) + 1; horizontal bars = floor((H − 2cover) ÷ spH) + 1; sum × kg/m.

Wall Rebar Calculator — FAQ

How do you calculate rebar for a wall?

Count vertical and horizontal bars from the spacing, then multiply lengths by unit weight.

What is rebar spacing in a block wall?

Often vertical bars at 400–800 mm and horizontal bars per course or bond beam — design governs.
