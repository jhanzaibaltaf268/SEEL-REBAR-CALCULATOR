Kako uporabljati: Kalkulator Armature Plošče

  1. Izberite premer palice in enote.
  2. Vnesite dimenzije in količino.
  3. Takoj odčitajte rezultat.

Formula

Bars one way = floor((dimension − 2 × cover) ÷ spacing) + 1, repeated for both directions. Total weight = total bar length × kg/m.
