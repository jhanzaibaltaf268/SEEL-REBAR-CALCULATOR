Kako uporabljati: Kalkulator Teže Armature

  1. Izberite premer palice in enote.
  2. Vnesite dimenzije in količino.

Formula

Weight per metre (kg) = d² ÷ 162.28, where d is the bar diameter in mm. Total = weight/m × length × quantity.
