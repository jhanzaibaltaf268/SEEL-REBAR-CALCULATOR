How to use: Concrete & Rebar Estimator

  1. Enter length, width and thickness.
  2. Set the steel ratio (kg per m³ of concrete).
  3. Read the volume and steel estimate.

Formula

Volume = L × W × thickness. Steel = volume × ratio (typically 80–120 kg/m³ for slabs).

Concrete & Rebar Estimator — FAQ

How much steel per m³ of concrete?

Roughly 80–120 kg/m³ for slabs, more for columns and beams — design governs.

How do I calculate concrete volume?

Multiply length × width × thickness in consistent units.
