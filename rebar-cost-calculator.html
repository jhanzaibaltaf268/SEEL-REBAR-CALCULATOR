How to use: Rebar Cost Calculator

  1. Enter the total rebar weight.
  2. Enter your price per kg.
  3. Add a wastage percentage to get the cost.

Formula

Cost = weight × (1 + wastage%) × price per kg.

Rebar Cost Calculator — FAQ

How much does rebar cost?

It varies by market and size; enter your local price per kg for an accurate estimate.

What wastage should I allow for rebar?

Typically 3–5% to cover offcuts and laps.
