Contact Us

Corrections, suggestions, and questions about the calculators — all welcome.

How to reach us

Email: mjzaib82@gmail.com

There is no contact form on this site, so email or phone are the ways to get in touch.

What to include in your message

The more specific you are, the faster we can help. Please include:

  • The calculator page you were using (for example, the concrete slab calculator).
  • The numbers you entered — dimensions, spacing, cover, bar size.
  • What result you got, and what you expected, if you are reporting a possible mistake.

What we can help with

  • Possible mistakes in a calculator's result or formula.
  • Suggestions for new calculators or improvements to existing ones.
  • Questions about how a calculator works or how to use it.

What we cannot do

We cannot provide structural design advice for a specific project — bar sizes, spacing, and cover for real construction must come from your structural drawings and a qualified engineer. The calculators are estimating tools, and their results should always be verified before building.

Before you write

Common questions — how slab rebar is counted, what spacing to use, whether laps are included — are already answered on the homepage FAQ and on each calculator's own FAQ section. A quick check there may save you the wait.

Privacy

Anything you send by email is used only to answer you. See the Privacy Policy for how this site handles data.
