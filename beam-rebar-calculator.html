How to use: Beam Rebar Calculator

  1. Enter beam length and cross-section.
  2. Set the number and size of top and bottom bars.
  3. Set stirrup spacing and bar size.

Formula

Main steel = (top + bottom bars) × length × kg/m. Stirrups = floor(length ÷ spacing) + 1 at the stirrup cutting length.

Beam Rebar Calculator — FAQ

How do you calculate beam reinforcement?

Add the main bar weight to the stirrup weight along the beam length.

What is the spacing of stirrups in a beam?

Often closer near supports; commonly 100–200 mm — design governs.
