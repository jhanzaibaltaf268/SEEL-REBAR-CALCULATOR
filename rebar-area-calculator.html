How to use: Rebar Area Calculator

  1. Pick the bar size and number of bars.
  2. Optionally enter the section width and depth.
  3. Read Ast and the steel percentage.

Formula

Area of one bar = π/4 × d². Ast = number of bars × area. Steel % = Ast ÷ (b × D) × 100.

Rebar Area Calculator — FAQ

What is the area of a 12mm rebar?

π/4 × 12² ≈ 113.1 mm² per bar.

What is Ast in reinforcement?

Ast is the total cross-sectional area of tension steel in a section.
