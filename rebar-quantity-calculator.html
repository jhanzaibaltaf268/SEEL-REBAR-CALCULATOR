How to use: Rebar Quantity & Takeoff Calculator

  1. Enter the area to be reinforced.
  2. Set the grid spacing (both ways).
  3. Pick the bar size for kg/m² and the total.

Formula

Bar length per m² = 2 ÷ spacing(m). Weight/m² = that × kg/m. Total = weight/m² × area.

Rebar Quantity & Takeoff Calculator — FAQ

How much rebar per square foot?

It depends on spacing and bar size; the tool gives kg/ft² for your inputs.

How do you do a rebar takeoff?

Estimate bar length per unit area from the spacing, then multiply by area and unit weight.
