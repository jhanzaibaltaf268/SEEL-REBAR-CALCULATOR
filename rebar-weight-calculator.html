How to use: Rebar Weight Calculator

  1. Choose the bar size (metric mm or US # size).
  2. Enter the length of one bar and how many pieces.
  3. Read the total weight in kg, lb and tons.

Formula

Weight per metre (kg) = d² ÷ 162.28, where d is the bar diameter in mm. Total = weight/m × length × quantity.

Rebar Weight Calculator — FAQ

What is the weight of a 12mm rebar?

A 12 mm bar weighs 12² ÷ 162.28 ≈ 0.888 kg per metre.

How do you calculate the weight of steel rebar?

Square the diameter in mm, divide by 162.28 to get kg/m, then multiply by length and quantity.
